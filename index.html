About

Tech, explained with a point of view

I'm Mohamed Elnahal, an Egyptian tech creator creating content that makes technology easier to understand and more interesting to explore.

Since 2021, I've built an engaged audience across YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram through honest opinions, tech storytelling, and visually-driven content covering smartphones, gadgets, and the technology shaping everyday life.

My goal is simple: make technology feel less complicated and a lot more interesting.

The kind of content brands ask to run again.
Straightforward reviews people trust, and results that bring companies back for a second and third collaboration.
